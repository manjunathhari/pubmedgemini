Get your free key in 3 steps

1

Open Google AI Studio and tap Create API key

Sign in with any Google account. If it asks for a project, choose one with billing turned off.

Opens in a new tab; this page stays open.

2

Tap Copy next to your new key

The key starts with AQ. or AIza.

3

Come back here and tap Paste

Your key is checked and saved straight away.

Key not accepted? Common fixes
"This key's project has billing turned on"
In AI Studio, tap Create API key again and pick a project with billing off (or create a new project).
"Google did not accept this key"
Copy it again with the Copy button; do not select it by hand.
"Used today's free limit"
It is saved and works again after the time shown.

Where your key is kept

Only in this browser, encrypted, and only for your Google sign-in. It stays until you remove it. Our server uses it while your question runs and never saves it.

On a new phone or another browser, paste it once more. Clearing this browser's data also removes it.

On Google's free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and its reviewers may read it. Do not type anything that identifies a patient. Google's Gemini API terms
